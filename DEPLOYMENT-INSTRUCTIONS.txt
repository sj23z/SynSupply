public/build/** in this package is already the compiled output (43

----------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 — REQUIRED: Verify
----------------------------------------------------

As Owner or Manager:
  - Open Reports -> Areas -> any area with customers. Confirm you see
    each customer individually (not one aggregate row), and that
    clicking a customer expands their full invoice/payment/return
    statement with color coding.
  - Open Reports -> Sales Representatives -> any rep with assigned
    customers. Same check.
  - Confirm the Area/Rep totals include Cash / Bank Transfer / Other /
    Settlement / Discount / Actual Cash Received breakdown.
  - Open both PDFs (Area, Sales Rep) and confirm they show the same
    per-customer detail as the screen, not just a summary table.
  - Open a Product report detail page and confirm Area/Rep/Discount/
    Returned columns appear.
  - Collections: create/edit a collection, confirm the Edit button
    works and the Dashboard's cash figure updates correctly.

As Manager specifically:
  - Confirm no COGS/cost/profit field appears anywhere in the Area,
    Sales Rep, or Product reports (only Owner should see them on the
    Product report).

On mobile:
  - Area/Sales-Rep report customer cards stack correctly, tables
    inside them scroll within their own container, no page-level
    horizontal scroll.

If any of the above fails, see ROLLBACK-INSTRUCTIONS.txt.
